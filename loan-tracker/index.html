Privacy and usage

No account, server or subscription is needed. Data is stored locally on this device. Deleting website data, reinstalling, or losing your phone can erase it. There is no automatic PIN, encryption or cloud sync. For best offline results, open the hosted app once while online, then add it to your Home Screen in Safari.
